A friendly macOS cleaner that shows you exactly what it found and moves junk to the Trash only after you review it. It also scans for known malware.

**Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later.** Universal: Apple Silicon and Intel.

## Install

1. Download **Filkollen-0.2.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag Filkollen to Applications.
2. Open Filkollen. This beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   - **macOS 15 Sequoia and later:** click *Done*, then open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the Filkollen message, and click **Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 13–14:** right-click Filkollen in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.

   You only do this once per version.
3. Follow the onboarding screen to grant **Full Disk Access**. Without it Filkollen can't see most of `~/Library`.

After updating to a new beta you may have to turn Full Disk Access off and on again for Filkollen, because unsigned builds get a new identity each version.

- **New look.** Every screen has been redesigned: a calmer layout, a live scan ring, clearer review sheets, light and dark mode, and smoother page switching.
- **More to clean.** New categories: Developer Junk, Old Installers, iOS Backups & Firmware, Browser & Mail Caches, Duplicate Files, and Large & Old Files.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/TheRoben-Dev/Filkollen/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Good to know

- Junk always goes to the Trash after review, so you can put it back. Only confirmed malware can be deleted permanently.
- The malware database (abuse.ch MalwareBazaar) needs a free Auth-Key, which you enter on the Malware Scan page.
- No telemetry. The only network traffic is the malware database download.

Check the download against the `.sha256` file: `shasum -a 256 Filkollen-0.2.0.dmg`
