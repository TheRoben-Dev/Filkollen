Filkollen gets a new look: dark, quiet and a little luxurious.

**Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later.** Universal: Apple Silicon and Intel.

## What's new

- **Noir.** Filkollen is now dark by default: an onyx background with a soft champagne glow, gold accents and buttons, gold-edged cards, and serif titles and figures. Prefer light? Settings › General › Appearance › Theme › Match System.
- **New icon.** "Halo": a champagne ring and star on matte black.
- **Menu bar panel fixed.** It's a rounded, solid panel again, without the square see-through sheet or the desktop showing around it.
- **Clearer at a glance.** Kept duplicate copies show a gold lock, Delete buttons are a calm ruby outline, and notes no longer use bright blue.
- **Fixes.** Apps with a blank name show up properly in App Uninstaller, and counts say "1 item".

Nothing changed in what Filkollen cleans or how: junk still goes to the Trash only after you review it.

## Install

1. Download **Filkollen-0.3.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag Filkollen to Applications (replace the old copy if asked).
2. This beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   - **macOS 15 Sequoia and later:** click *Done*, then open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the Filkollen message, and click **Open Anyway**.
   - **macOS 13–14:** right-click Filkollen in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open**.
3. If scans suddenly find much less, turn **Full Disk Access** for Filkollen off and on again in System Settings › Privacy & Security; unsigned builds get a new identity each version.

If the Dock or Launchpad still shows the old icon, make sure only one Filkollen is in Applications, then log out and back in.

Check the download against the `.sha256` file: `shasum -a 256 Filkollen-0.3.0.dmg`
